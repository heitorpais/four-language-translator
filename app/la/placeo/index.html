placeo

/ˈpɫa.ke.oː/ verb A1 (estimated)

1. to be pleasing or agreeable to; to please, to be nice; to be welcome or acceptable; to satisfy, suit (in a mental rather than a sensual way)

Englishto please, to suit, to satisfy

2. of an accomplishment, e.g. writing, acting, artistry, etc: to please, to win applause, to earn approval or other favorable response

3. to like (the dative should be translated as the subject in English)
